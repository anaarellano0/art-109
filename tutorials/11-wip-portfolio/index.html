About Me

Sofia is a multimedia artist currently pursuing her BFA in Digital Media Arts at San Jose State University. She's constantly working to improve her creative practice, inspired from a young age by the art of storytelling; she moves through her practice as she does with life, "with an open heart". Sofia works with software like the Adobe Creative Cloud, including Illustrator, Photoshop, After Effects, Lightroom, and Premiere Pro; she illustrates and designs digitally with a number of these software programs as well as Procreate. She has a soft background in 3D design software like Maya and Blender. In her professional and personal practice, she follows the words of Professor Keating: “No matter what anybody tells you, words and ideas can change the world.”

Thank you so much for stopping by!
